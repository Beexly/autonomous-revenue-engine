The Meta Tracking Audit Workbook

Find and fix the Pixel + CAPI defects inflating your cost-per-result — with evidence you can verify in your own Events Manager. For stores running Meta ads. ~90 minutes start to finish.

How to use: work top to bottom. Every check has a Pass / Fail / N-A cell and an evidence cell — fill them in. At the end you'll have a prioritized fix list and a before-state record so you can prove improvement after fixes. Nothing here requires code access to start; items marked 🔧 need your developer or app settings to fix.

1.2 Dedup verification. Open each "Both" event → look for deduplication stats. Meta dedupes only on matching event_name + event_id. If dedup shows 0% or is absent while both channels fire, you are double-counting — every reported Purchase may be two. 🔧 Fix: one event_id generated per user action, passed to BOTH the Pixel call and the CAPI payload. Classic failure: platform-native Pixel (e.g. Shopify) + a separate CAPI app, each inventing its own ids.
