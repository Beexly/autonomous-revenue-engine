# Adversarial Review Protocol

Every content asset and major system decision is red-teamed before exposure.

## Content-Level Attacks
- Simulated platform classifier (originality, automation, engagement-bait)
- Synthetic skeptical Premium user
- Competitive undercutting ("why is this better than existing high-signal accounts?")
- Policy risk scan against current X Original Content Rewards language

## System-Level Attacks
- Over-optimization traps
- Detection risk from posting patterns
- Dependency on any single platform or revenue stream
- Bandwidth / usage burn relative to signal produced

## Pass Criteria
Asset or decision must survive the attack set without critical failure. Failures are rewritten or killed.

## Logging
Every review is logged with attack vectors used and outcome. Patterns of failure feed back into the originality gate and content system.
