Forecast

Every number in the forecast comes from deals you closed yourself. Nothing here is scored by a built-in “likely to close” percentage. The one fixed table on the page is the CRM comparison figure under “How this range is made”, and it is labelled there as the author’s guess rather than your record. Where your own record is too thin to support a number, the page says so and prints the reason instead.

Nothing to forecast yet

This page reads the deal book on the Pipeline page. It needs two things from it: deals you have open now, and deals you have already closed — won or lost. Losses matter as much as wins; a forecast built only from wins reads your pipeline as certain.

Add your deals on the Pipeline page, and mark each one won or lost as it settles. Come back here once you have some closed history and this page will tell you what it can support.
