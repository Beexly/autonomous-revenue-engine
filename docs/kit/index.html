What's the difference between the one-pager and the full website?
The one-pager is a single screen: your name, services, and a big call button — $350 flat. The full website is up to 5 pages with a quote form, your Google reviews, maps, and SEO baked in — $900 flat. Same deal on both: free preview first, you own it, no monthly fees.
What kind of workflows do you build?
The boring stuff that loses you money: missed calls that never get a text back, leads nobody follows up with, happy customers nobody asks for a review. DM “WORKFLOW” and tell me what's eating your time — I'll tell you straight if I can automate it and what it'll cost.
How long does it take?
The preview usually lands the same day you send your info. Once you approve it, the site goes live within a few days.
What do you need from me?
Four things: your business name, your phone number, the services you want listed, and the towns you serve. If you have a logo or photos, wonderful — send them. If not, the page works fine without them.
What if I hate the preview?
Then you pay nothing and we're done — no hard feelings, no invoice, no follow-up sales pitch. The preview is free because I'd rather you see it than take my word for it.
How do I pay the $350?
We sort that out when you approve the preview — whatever's easiest for you. You don't pay anything until you've seen your page and said yes.
Who owns the website?
You do. It's built for you, by hand, yours to keep. There are no monthly fees and nothing to cancel.
Can you change things later?
Small stuff — a new phone number, a service added or removed — just message me and I'll update it. If you ever want a second page or something bigger, we'll talk about what that costs before I touch anything.
Do you build bigger websites?
Yes — the full website tier is up to 5 pages (home, services, about, reviews, contact) with a quote form that sends leads to your phone, your Google reviews and maps embedded, and basic SEO — $900 flat. Same deal: free homepage preview first, you own it, no monthly fees.
