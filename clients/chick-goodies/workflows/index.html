The three things that
happen after the party.

Your website answers the question "who caters this?". None of it answers "did that enquiry ever get a reply?" or "did that host ever leave a review?" Those two questions are where the next bookings come from, and right now nothing is asking them.

This page is for Tricia and Garrett. It is not part of the website and is not linked from it.

01 · Where it stands today

The money path works.
It just stops at the party.

  1. A host finds the siteSearch, The Knot, or a friend.
  2. They send the quoteOpens a text or an email to Tricia with the date, headcount and table already written in.
  3. Tricia repliesPrice and availability, from her phone.
  4. The eventTable styled on site, ninety minutes of service.
  5. Then nothing No thank-you. No review asked for. No reason to come back. And no record anywhere of whether that first enquiry ever got answered.

Every event that finishes without a review ask is a ranking signal she never gets back. She has 13 reviews on The Knot and 11 on WeddingWire, both 5.0. On Google — the one people actually search — her profile still needs claiming, so none of that 5.0 is doing any work.

02 · What gets added

Three workflows. One event.

Press play. This is one real booking moving through the system, day by day. Nothing here sends itself without Tricia — every message opens on her phone with the words already written, and she presses send.

Day 0 — the enquiry arrives

  1. Day 0Enquiry landsDate, headcount, table and town arrive on her phone in a fixed order. It is logged, with a clock running on it.
  2. Day 0 + 4hUnanswered checkIf it has not been replied to, Tricia gets one nudge. This is the whole of "did that enquiry get an answer?" — and it is the cheapest booking she will ever make.
  3. Day 2–40Quote, booking, the eventUnchanged. She prices by hand and takes payment her own way.
  4. Event + 2 daysThank-you, with a photoOne message, one photo from their table. It costs nothing and it is the reason the next one works.
  5. Event + 3 daysThe review askOne tap, straight to her Google review form. This is the single highest-value line on this page.
  6. Event + 30 daysThe returnA seasonal note to a host who already knows the food is good. The cheapest customer is one who has already paid you once.

Workflow one

Nothing falls in a hole

An enquiry is logged the second it is sent, and it stays open until it is answered. One nudge at four hours.

What it changes: the enquiries she has already earned stop leaking. No new traffic required.

Workflow two

Every event asks for a review

Thank-you at day two with a photo, the Google review link at day three. One tap for the host.

What it changes: Google's local results lean hard on review count and how recently they arrived. This is the lever for "charcuterie catering near me".

Workflow three

Past hosts come back

A seasonal note at day thirty to someone who has already tasted the food.

What it changes: repeat and referral work, which cost nothing to reach, instead of only chasing strangers.

04 · Scope

What this is, and what it is not.

Included

  • Enquiry logged and tracked to an answer, with one nudge
  • Thank-you and review-ask messages, written, on her phone, one tap
  • Google review link that goes straight to the form
  • The day-thirty return note
  • A one-screen monthly report: calls, quotes, bookings, best photo

Deliberately not included

  • No chatbot and no automated replies. Every message is hers, and she presses send.
  • No online payment, no deposits, no booking calendar. She prices by hand and takes payment her own way.
  • No paid SMS service. It opens the app already on her phone.
  • No AI writing to her customers.
