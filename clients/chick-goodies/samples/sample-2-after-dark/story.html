For more than 35 years, Tricia Holfelder has worked both front-of-house and back-of-house in the restaurant industry. That experience sits behind every table she prepares.

Her approach is personal: one party at a time, with scratch-made breads, jellies and jams. From grazing tables to charcuterie carts, the food is part of the occasion — not an afterthought.

Based in Tomball and serving The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, Tricia offers hospitality in English and Spanish.
