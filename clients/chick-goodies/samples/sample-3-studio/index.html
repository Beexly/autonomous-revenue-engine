From kitchen dreams to your table.

06 — Before you book

What people ask.

What is the minimum guest count?

Fifty guests for the per-person tables, $24 to $38 a person. The Holy Grail of Grazing is priced by the table: $2,000 feeds 75 guests, $3,500 feeds 150.

Are there setup fees or tax?

Yes — 18% tax and a $229 flat setup fee on every grazing table. Each service runs ninety minutes, and extra time is $3 per person per half hour.

Where do you set up?

Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, out of the kitchen at 11931 Brantley Haven Drive, Tomball TX 77375.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks is comfortable for most events. Call or text to ask about sooner — dates do open up.

Can you match a theme or handle dietary needs?

Yes. Colours, seasonal fruit and styling can be matched to your event, and swaps are worked out when you book.
