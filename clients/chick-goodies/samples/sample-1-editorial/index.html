About Tricia Holfelder

From kitchen dreams to your table.

With more than 35 years in the restaurant industry, Tricia Holfelder is known for her passion, precision and unwavering commitment to hospitality. She started in restaurants and rose through the ranks — front-of-house to back-of-house — before building Charcuterie Chick around the part she loves most: feeding a room full of people who came to celebrate something.

Every table is hers. Sourced, styled and served by the same hands that have been doing this for three and a half decades.

What is the minimum guest count?

Fifty guests for the per-person tables, $24 to $38 a person. The Holy Grail is $2,000 for 75 guests and $3,500 for 150.

Are there setup fees or tax?

18% tax and a $229 flat setup fee. Ninety minutes of service. Extra time $3 per person per half hour.

Where do you set up?

Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, out of 11931 Brantley Haven Drive, Tomball TX 77375.
