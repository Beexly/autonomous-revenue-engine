No. Once you send the draft from your own email or phone, I receive your note. I check the date, we talk through the menu, and I send you a real quote. Nothing is held until we've agreed it.

How much does a grazing table cost?

$24 to $38 a person depending on the table, for 50 guests or more. Add the $229 setup and 18% tax. The Holy Grail is $2,000 for 75 guests or $3,500 for 150.

We're fewer than 50. What then?

Ask me about boards, the cart, sweets and sips. Boards are priced on request.

Where do you go?

Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, and up to 100 miles out.
