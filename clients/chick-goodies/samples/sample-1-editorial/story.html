How I got here

Front of the house, then the back, then both.

I spent more than 35 years in restaurants. I've worked the floor and I've worked the line. Charcuterie Chick is what I do with all of that.

Now I run Houston's largest charcuterie cart. The breads, jellies and jams are made from scratch. I take one party at a time.

Woman-owned. Based in Tomball, cooking for The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe.
