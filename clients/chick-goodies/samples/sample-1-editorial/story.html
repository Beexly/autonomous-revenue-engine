From the kitchen to your company

It starts with food.
It stays with people.

More than 35 years in the restaurant industry. Both sides of the kitchen door.

Tricia’s experience spans front-of-house and back-of-house: the food on the plate and the hospitality around it. Charcuterie Chick brings those worlds together through grazing tables, boards, carts and catering.

Based in Tomball and serving The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, this woman-owned business takes its approach one party at a time.
