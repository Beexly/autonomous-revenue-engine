# Garden Atelier

Art direction: pistachio daylight across an aubergine editorial garden; enormous italic type surrounds oval photographic specimens, with caustic light moving over a non-food table surface.

## Identity and constraints
- DNA: ../../FACTS.md and ../shared/facts.json. All menu names, prices and contacts generated from JSON; no inferred availability or booking.
- Source images: local img only, original client photography. Original brand.webp on cream mounting in header/footer; 210px desktop, 168px mobile. No alternate/retyped logos.
- Shot list: knot-hero/2/3/4 (945×720) for wide arrangements; cart-640 (640×480); catering-1000 (1000×560); tricia-662 (662×823); graze-01/02, board-01, table-01/02, sips-01, sweets-01 (480×640). Width never exceeds native pixels.
- Five static routes with independent local garden.css/garden.js, no framework, tracking, paid services or automatic sending/storage.
- Progressive enhancement: all menu facts and gallery image links are native HTML, draft form enhances direct mail/text contact. Procedural backdrop is decorative only; reduced motion, pause, hidden-tab stop, context-loss fallback.

## Sequence
Structure and type first; WebGL and interactions second; real Playwright local-server tests on 360/390/768/1440 plus motion, no-JS and context loss. Capture screenshots without claiming visual approval. No deployment, git index changes or work outside assigned sample and qa-garden.py.
