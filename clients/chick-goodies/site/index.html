Questions

What people ask before booking.

What is the minimum guest count for a grazing table?

Fifty guests for the per-person tables ($24 to $38 a person). The Holy Grail of Grazing is priced by the table instead — $2,000 feeds 75 guests, $3,500 feeds 150.

Are there setup fees or tax on top of the table price?

Yes. Every grazing table carries 18% tax and a $229 flat setup fee. Each table service runs 90 minutes, and extra time can be added for $3 per person per half hour.

Where do you deliver and set up?

Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring and Conroe, out of the kitchen at 11931 Brantley Haven Drive, Tomball TX 77375.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks is comfortable for most events. Call or text to ask about sooner — dates do open up.

Can you match a theme or handle dietary needs?

Yes. Colors, seasonal fruit and styling can be matched to your event, and swaps are worked out when you book — call or text with the details.
