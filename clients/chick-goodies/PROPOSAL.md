## Page 3. The workflows ($500)

Headline: *Six texts follow one booking. You tap send.*

Explained up top, in two columns. Left, "What a workflow is": a message that
is ready the moment something happens (a quote lands, an event ends, a season
nears); we write each one with her, in her voice, and put it on her phone; on
the day her phone reminds her; she reads it and taps send; nothing goes out on
its own and nothing sounds like a robot. Value line: *One saved quote pays for
it twice.* A grazing table starts at $1,200 (50 guests at $24); the package is
under half of one 50-guest table. Right, "$500, what it buys": six routines on
her phone · every message written with her, in her voice · a reminder on the
day · the log (every quote request, and whether it was answered) · one screen
a month (calls, quotes, bookings, best photo) · yours for good: no monthly fee,
no software to learn, no chatbot.

Then the six routines, drawn as one phone thread (left, dark panel: the
incoming quote request, then five outgoing texts in her voice with [first
name], [date], [time], [offer], [link] as placeholders, each under a timing
stamp) beside a numbered day line (right: routine name and one why-line).
