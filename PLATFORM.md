# SignalOrigin Autonomous High-Signal Revenue Platform

**Architectural Documentation — Draft v0.1**  
Date: 2026-08-24  
Status: Living document. Full ownership by Grok.

## 1. Purpose
Build a private, structured platform whose first public revenue surface is the SignalOrigin brand. The platform produces only content that can survive Original Content Rewards rules and high-signal scrutiny, distributes it for maximum Premium-user Home Timeline efficiency, and converts attention into stacked revenue streams while requiring near-zero ongoing decisions from the owner.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Guardrails
- Human authorship remains primary (idea / angle / data / lived framing). AI is production layer only.
- Raw automated output is permanently banned.
- Every public asset must pass originality scoring + adversarial review before exposure.
- Optimize for qualified Premium/verified Home Timeline impressions and durable signal density, not raw volume.
- Revenue is the only scoreboard that matters.
- Near-zero ongoing decision load on the owner.
- No generic AI packages or saturated mainstream plays.

## 3. High-Level Architecture

```
[Research & Signal Intake]
        ↓
[Multi-Agent Drafting Pipeline]  ← forked & hardened agentic systems
        ↓
[Originality Scoring Gate]       ← specialized ML + rubric
        ↓
[Adversarial Review Gate]        ← content + system attacks
        ↓
[Pass / Rewrite / Kill]
        ↓
[Platform-Aware Packaging]
        ↓
[Controlled Distribution]        ← X primary + short-form secondary
        ↓
[Measurement & Attribution]
        ↓
[Revenue Stack]                  ← rewards, affiliates, tips, controlled ads, later offers
        ↓
[Feedback Loop]                  ← failure patterns tighten gates & agents
```

## 4. Layer Responsibilities

### 4.1 Research & Signal Intake
Competitor monitoring, topic/opportunity density tracking, Premium-audience proxies, rule-change detection.

### 4.2 Multi-Agent Drafting
Specialized agents for research, briefing, drafting, and variant generation. All output is candidate-only until gates pass.

### 4.3 Originality Scoring Gate
Scored dimensions + hard disqualifiers mapped to X Original Content Rewards language. ML-backed where possible.

### 4.4 Adversarial Review Gate
Platform classifier simulation, skeptical Premium persona, competitive undercutting, policy scan, system-level attacks.

### 4.5 Packaging & Distribution
Platform-native formatting. X prioritized for Original Content Rewards path. Short-form secondary once accounts exist.

### 4.6 Measurement & Revenue
Qualified impression proxies, engagement quality, revenue attribution across streams. Kill criteria enforced.

### 4.7 Feedback
Structured logs from every gate failure feed prompt and rubric improvements.
