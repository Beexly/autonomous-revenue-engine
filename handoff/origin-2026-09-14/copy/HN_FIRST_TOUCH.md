## Comment draft (do not post; no links)

The interesting claim here isn't that Claude has a tell. Everyone with a highlighter already knew that. It's that Anthropic's own public first screen still refuses to ship it. I run a quality gate on outbound copy: a local check that scores and does not write the post, plus a kill log of phrases that die before anything goes out. The log is almost entirely the markers this post names — the "you're not relearning X, you're just using a cleaner Y" antithesis, punchy eyeball-kick sentences, load-bearing applied to things that are not load-bearing, and the three-pass outline that restates the thesis before it has one. A 0.5-spread on those collocations is enough; you do not need a statistical detector, which is why matching on pre-Claude-rare phrase patterns works. If the lab that lets Claude write most of its code still copy-edits Constitution and research prose down to something a human would sign, that is a gate, not a taste argument. The rest of us are late to applying the same rule to anything that has to survive a first screen.
