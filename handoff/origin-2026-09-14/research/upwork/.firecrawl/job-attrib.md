# Marketing Analytics & Attribution Specialist — GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel/CAPI

Posted yesterday

Worldwide

**Summary**

Santa’s Secret Workshop is looking for an expert Marketing Analytics & Attribution Specialist to repair and build a reliable, founder-owned measurement system across our marketing funnel.

We recently completed a performance marketing audit that identified significant tracking issues in our current setup. We are not looking for someone to run ads or develop marketing strategy. We are looking for a technical analytics specialist who can take the existing audit findings, implement the necessary fixes, build a clean attribution framework, test everything thoroughly, and document the system for future use.

Current issues already identified

Our audit found, among other things:

\\* A Meta Lead event is currently firing on homepage/page views rather than actual lead submissions.
\\* Purchases are not being properly tracked as Purchase events with revenue/value and currency.
\\* Lead-to-paid conversion therefore cannot currently be measured reliably.
\\* Meta Conversions API/server-side tracking needs to be verified and configured correctly, including deduplication where applicable.
\\* UTM tagging is not standardized across our marketing channels.
\\* We need reliable attribution across the customer journey rather than relying on individual advertising-platform reporting.

You will receive the existing audit findings, so you will not be starting from scratch.

What we need built

The goal is to create a measurement foundation that allows us to answer:

Where did a visitor/customer come from → what did they do → did they become a legitimate lead → did they purchase → how much revenue did they generate?

The system should be designed to accommodate current and future acquisition channels, including:

\\* Email marketing
\\* Organic social
\\* Meta advertising
\\* Future paid media
\\* Partnerships/referrals
\\* PR
\\* QR codes/offline campaigns
\\* Other future acquisition sources

Core deliverables

1\. Analytics & tracking audit/cleanup

\\* Review the existing GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and conversion setup.
\\* Confirm what is currently working, broken, duplicated, or missing.
\\* Preserve what is functioning correctly rather than rebuilding unnecessarily.

2\. Conversion tracking implementation

\\* Correct Lead tracking so events fire only on legitimate lead/form submissions.
\\* Implement and validate Purchase tracking.
\\* Pass purchase value/revenue and currency correctly.
\\* Configure other funnel events where appropriate.
\\* Verify tracking through controlled test submissions/test purchases.

3\. Meta tracking

\\* Verify and repair Meta Pixel implementation.
\\* Verify/configure Meta Conversions API where appropriate.
\\* Ensure browser/server events are properly deduplicated.
\\* Validate events through Meta Events Manager.

4\. GA4 + Google Tag Manager

\\* Ensure GA4 and GTM are configured cleanly.
\\* Establish a logical event/conversion architecture that can be maintained and expanded.
\\* QA using appropriate debugging/testing tools.

5\. UTM & attribution framework

\\* Create a standardized UTM naming convention for email, organic social, paid media, partnerships, QR codes, referrals, and future campaigns.
\\* Provide a simple system/SOP so future team members and contractors use UTMs consistently.

6\. Founder-friendly reporting

\\* Create a clear reporting/dashboard solution that allows a non-technical founder to understand key acquisition and conversion performance.
\\* At minimum, reporting should make it possible to evaluate traffic/source, leads, purchases, revenue, conversion rates, and channel/campaign performance.
\\* The reporting architecture should be expandable as new channels are introduced.

7\. QA + documentation + handoff

\\* Thoroughly test the implementation before completion.
\\* Provide clear documentation of what was changed, how the tracking architecture works, naming conventions, events/conversions, and how future specialists should maintain or expand it.
\\* Conduct a final handoff/walkthrough.

What success looks like

At the end of this engagement, Santa’s Secret Workshop should own a reliable measurement system that allows us to make marketing decisions using our own data.

We should be able to determine which channels and campaigns generate real leads, paying customers, revenue, conversion rates, and eventually customer acquisition costs/ROAS when paid campaigns are running.

The system should not depend on the original freelancer remaining involved indefinitely. Documentation and clean handoff are important.

Ideal candidate

We are looking for someone with strong hands-on experience in:

\\* Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
\\* Google Tag Manager
\\* Meta Pixel
\\* Meta Conversions API
\\* Conversion/event tracking
\\* UTM architecture and attribution
\\* Marketing dashboards/reporting
\\* QA/debugging of analytics implementations

Experience working with small businesses/e-commerce or booking/reservation funnels is strongly preferred.

Just as important as technical ability: we value excellent organization, concise communication, proactive problem-solving, and exceptionally clear documentation.

When applying

Please answer the following:

1\. Briefly describe a recent GA4/GTM + Meta tracking implementation you personally completed.
2\. If a Meta Lead event is firing on every homepage visit and no Purchase event exists, how would you approach diagnosing and correcting the setup?
3\. How would you structure attribution/UTMs so email, organic social, paid media, partnerships, QR codes, and future channels can be compared consistently?
4\. What tools/process do you use to QA and validate Lead and Purchase events?
5\. What documentation and handoff materials would you provide when the project is complete?
6\. Based on the scope above, identify anything you believe may require access, integration work, or additional technical discovery before you can guarantee implementation.

This is a fixed-price implementation project with a $1,000 posted budget. We are open to proposals below the posted budget based on your assessment of the work.
