**Summary**

We’re looking for a technical tracking expert to help us fix a Meta conversion tracking issue connected to SamCart.

The current problem: failed payment attempts in SamCart are being tracked in Meta as successful Purchase conversions. We need someone to identify exactly where the Purchase event is firing incorrectly and fix the setup so Meta only records completed/successful purchases.

From what we understand, this may be caused by SamCart’s built-in pixel firing on the order attempt, or by duplicate Purchase events being sent through the thank-you page and/or CAPI.

You should be very experienced with:
→ Google Tag Manager
→ Meta Pixel + Conversions API
→ Meta Events Manager
→ Event deduplication
→ Browser + server-side tracking
→ SamCart or similar checkout platforms
→ GA4 / conversion tracking
→ Debugging custom event setups

This is the first issue we need fixed, but we’re looking for someone long-term who can help our clients with tracking setup, troubleshooting, Pixel/CAPI issues, and conversion tracking across different funnels and platforms.

Please include examples of similar tracking issues you’ve diagnosed and fixed.
