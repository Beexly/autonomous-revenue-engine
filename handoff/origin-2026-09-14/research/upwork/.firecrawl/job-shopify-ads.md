# Shopify / Meta Ads E-commerce Specialist – Generate Online Sales

Posted yesterday

Worldwide

**Summary**

I have recently launched CampYard, a niche Australian equine e-commerce business selling a portable horse yard system through Shopify.

The product is established, the website is live and I have existing photography, video and social media content. I am now looking for an experienced e-commerce performance marketer who can help turn this into online sales.

The objective is simple: generate profitable online sales.

This is not a social media management role and I am not looking for someone simply to increase followers, engagement or post content.

Initially I am looking for someone who can:

Review the existing Shopify store and product purchase journey
Ensure Meta Pixel, Conversions API and purchase tracking are correctly configured
Develop and run Meta sales campaigns across Facebook and Instagram
Set up effective retargeting
Identify and test appropriate audiences
Test different advertising creative and messaging
Review the product page and recommend practical improvements to increase conversion
Track advertising spend against actual orders and revenue
Report clearly on CAC, conversion rate and ROAS

CampYard sells a physical product for AUD $890, primarily targeting Australian horse owners involved in campdrafting, polocrosse, rodeo, pony club, endurance, equestrian competition and travelling with horses.

I understand the horse industry and can provide the product knowledge, photography, video and content. I need someone who understands the digital side and knows how to convert that into customers.

As a new business, my initial budget is modest. I would therefore prefer to start with a small fixed-price project/trial rather than a large monthly retainer.

If we can establish a customer acquisition model that generates profitable sales, I am very willing to reinvest and progressively increase the advertising and marketing budget.

When applying, please provide:

Examples of Shopify or physical-product e-commerce businesses you have worked with.
Actual examples of Meta advertising results you have achieved, including advertising spend, revenue/sales generated, CAC and ROAS where possible.
What you would do in the first 30 days for a new Shopify store like CampYard.
Your recommended initial Meta advertising budget.
Your proposed fee for the initial project.

Experience with Australian e-commerce businesses would be highly regarded. Experience with equine, rural, agricultural, camping or outdoor markets would be an additional advantage.

Please don't send a generic agency proposal. I am particularly interested in hearing how you would approach generating the first consistent stream of online sales for this business.
