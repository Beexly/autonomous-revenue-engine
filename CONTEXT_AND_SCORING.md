## 1. Original Context Absorbed (Adult Vertical Explicitly Excluded)

The set-aside project was a **next-man-up content generator**:

- Influencer agents are spun up from pitched ideas.
- Each agent runs its own projects start-to-finish.
- Verticals can be anything: fitness, cooking, health, sports, politics, opinionated or passive, blue/red, lifestyle, etc.
- If one agent or surface is taken down, another steps forward.
- The train never stops rolling.

This is a resilience + coverage architecture: multiple parallel high-signal (or targeted) content lines under shared quality and revenue discipline, not a single brittle account.

### Alignment with SignalOrigin Platform

SignalOrigin is the **first public high-signal surface** and the current revenue focus. The broader platform architecture already supports the next-man-up pattern:

- Multiple candidate content lines / agent personas can be drafted under the same originality + adversarial gates.
- Shared Security, Learning, and Measurement seats apply across all lines.
- If one surface or persona loses eligibility or traction, capacity shifts to the next viable line without stopping the overall system.
- Human-authorship primary + AI production remains non-negotiable for every line that touches Original Content Rewards or public platforms.

We do not dilute SignalOrigin’s brand purity. We treat the next-man-up model as the long-term organizational pattern the platform is designed to support once the first surface is producing measurable revenue.

## 2. Revenue Path Scoring (Engineered Direction)

Every content candidate and every agent/line carries an explicit **revenue path score** before significant production resources are spent.

### Core dimensions
- **Expected value (EV)** — estimated contribution to Original Content Rewards, affiliates, tips, or later offers.
- **Cash speed** — time-to-first-measurable-revenue signal.
- **Risk** — policy, originality, detection, and brand risk.
- **Eligibility fit** — alignment with current Original Content Rewards rules and Premium-impression economics.
- **Marginal cost** — usage / production cost relative to expected return.

### Practical scoring approach
- Lightweight multi-factor score (inspired by ICE / performance-tier models): Impact × Confidence × Ease, adapted to our constraints, plus an explicit Risk penalty.
- Assets and lines are tiered (A / B / C). A-tier receives priority production and amplification. C-tier is killed or deferred.
- Only gate-cleared assets enter distribution; revenue-path score decides *which* cleared assets get scarce attention and budget first.
- Real revenue evidence (platform rewards, Stripe, tips, etc.) is the only ground truth that updates the scoring model over time (Learning loop).

This replaces volume-first or “make content for the sake of content” prioritization.

## 3. Sheaf Theory — Relevance to This Project

Sheaf theory formalizes **local-to-global consistency**: data or models that look fine locally may fail to glue into a coherent global section. Project-Tree’s CRC work makes this concrete with repair classes:

- **consistent** — sources agree under the maps
- **statistical** — maps OK, values drift
- **structural** — maps themselves cannot glue (non-trivial holonomy)
- **self_consistent_wrong_vs_ledger** — sources agree with each other but disagree with a system of record

### Direct applications to SignalOrigin / multi-agent platform

1. **Multi-source research integrity**  
   Research packets drawn from multiple sources can be checked for consistency before they enter drafting. Statistical drift or structural disagreement is flagged rather than silently averaged.

2. **Multi-agent / multi-line consistency**  
   When multiple influencer agents or content lines operate, claims, voice, and factual assertions can be treated as local sections. Sheaf-style checks detect when they are locally coherent but globally inconsistent or wrong relative to a source-of-truth.

3. **Security & Learning seats**  
   Repair-class thinking informs how we categorize gate failures and choose responses (realign, drop, refuse to “fix” by averaging). Coordinated false agreement is treated as a first-class risk.

4. **System resilience (next-man-up)**  
   The platform itself can be viewed as a distributed system whose local agents must remain consistent with global guardrails (authorship, originality, policy). Obstructions to global sections become explicit signals that a line or process needs repair or replacement rather than silent continuation.

We do not import the full Project-Tree mathematical surface as a hard dependency. We adopt the **consistency and repair-class mindset** as part of Security and Learning doctrine.
