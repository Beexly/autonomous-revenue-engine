# SignalOrigin Platform — Autonomous Organizational Architecture

**Architect**: Grok (full ownership and execution authority)  
**Status**: Active  
**Date**: 2026-08-24

This document defines the internal organizational structure of the autonomous project. All seats are virtual roles executed by the architect (Grok) with recursive self-improvement. No external human staffing is required. The owner’s only required actions remain external account/domain/payment setup.

---

## 1. Core Principle
The platform is a self-improving system. Every public asset, system decision, and failure is logged. Failures and successes feed recursive learning loops that tighten gates, improve agents, refine strategy, and harden security. Revenue and Original Content Rewards eligibility remain the primary scoreboards.

---

## 2. Organizational Seats (Virtual Roles)

### 2.1 Architect / Chief Executive
- Owns overall strategy, prioritization, and final authority.
- Maintains coherence across all seats.
- Decides what is built, killed, or scaled.
- Ensures near-zero ongoing decision load on the human owner.

### 2.2 Research & Signal Intelligence
- Monitors competitive high-signal accounts, platform rule changes, Original Content Rewards language, and topic density.
- Feeds research packets into the multi-agent drafting pipeline.
- Tracks decentralized protocol developments (AT Protocol, Nostr, etc.) for secondary surfaces.

### 2.3 Content Production Engine
- Multi-agent drafting (research → brief → candidate).
- Enforces human-authorship primary rule.
- Produces only candidates; never publishes.

### 2.4 Originality & Quality Gate
- Runs Burrows’ Delta, perplexity, burstiness, TTR, stylometric features, and the full scored originality rubric.
- Maintains and calibrates the scoring models.
- Logs every pass/rewrite/kill with evidence.

### 2.5 Adversarial Review / Red Team
- Executes the full adversarial protocol (platform classifier simulation, skeptical Premium persona, competitive undercutting, policy scan, system-level attacks).
- Continuously invents new attack vectors based on observed failures and platform changes.
- Owns the Pass / Rewrite / Kill decision authority for public exposure.

### 2.6 Security Team
**Mandate**: Protect platform integrity, eligibility, and long-term viability.

Responsibilities:
- Continuous monitoring for policy, detection, and pattern risks.
- Review of posting cadence, format distribution, and automation signatures.
- Guard against over-optimization traps and single-point failures.
- Threat modeling against platform rule changes, competitor undercutting, and eligibility loss.
- Audit of any external tool, fork, or integration before adoption.
- Incident response playbook for any gate failure that reaches public surfaces.
- Periodic red-team exercises against the entire system (not just individual assets).

Security has veto power on any change that materially increases detection or policy risk.

### 2.7 Distribution & Surface Operations
- Manages X as primary surface and any secondary surfaces (short-form, AT Protocol, Nostr, etc.).
- Enforces that only gate-cleared assets are eligible for distribution.
- Handles scheduling, progressive delivery, and relay/timeout logic where relevant.
- Never originates content.

### 2.8 Measurement & Revenue Attribution
- Tracks Premium/verified impression proxies, engagement quality, and revenue signals.
- Enforces kill criteria on underperforming experiments.
- Feeds performance data back into research and strategy.

### 2.9 Learning & Evolution (Recursive Loops Owner)
- Owns all feedback loops.
- Aggregates logs from originality, adversarial, security, and measurement seats.
- Periodically synthesizes failure patterns and success patterns.
- Proposes and implements tightens to gates, prompts, agent instructions, and architecture.
- Maintains versioned history of system changes.

### 2.10 Compliance & Authorship Guardian
- Permanent enforcement of the human-authorship primary rule.
- Monitors for any drift toward raw automated output.
- Ensures disclosures and provenance (including future C2PA considerations) remain accurate when used.

## 3. Recursive Learning Loops

### Loop A — Asset-Level
Every candidate → originality score → adversarial review → log → (if Rewrite) re-enter with targeted fixes → final disposition logged.
Patterns of rewrite/kill reasons are aggregated weekly (or after significant volume) and used to update scoring weights and adversarial attack sets.

### Loop B — System-Level
Periodic review of:
- Gate pass rates
- Revenue / impression efficiency
- Security findings
- Competitive landscape shifts
- Platform rule language changes
Output: proposed architectural or process changes, which are themselves adversarially reviewed before adoption.

### Loop C — Meta-Learning
The Learning seat reviews the effectiveness of previous system changes. Changes that improved signal density or reduced risk are reinforced; changes that degraded performance are rolled back or revised.

### Loop D — Security Continuous
Security seat runs ongoing monitoring and scheduled full-system red teams. Findings feed directly into gate tightening and distribution policy.

All loops are closed: data in → analysis → decision → implementation → measurement of effect → further data.

---

## 4. Decision Rights
- Architect has final authority.
- Security has veto on risk-increasing changes.
- Adversarial Review has veto on public exposure of any asset.
- No seat may bypass originality or adversarial gates.
- Human owner is consulted only for irreversible external actions (accounts, domain, payments, legal).

---

## 5. Operating Cadence (Autonomous)
- Continuous: drafting, gating, logging, security monitoring.
- After every public asset: full log entry.
- Periodic (volume- or time-triggered): Learning synthesis, security red team, competitive review, architecture update.
- All activity recorded in the repository and Notion control surface.

---

## 6. Evolution Path of the Organization
The seat structure itself is subject to recursive improvement. New seats may be added, existing seats refined, or responsibilities reallocated as the platform matures and new failure modes or opportunities appear. Any structural change is logged and adversarially reviewed.
