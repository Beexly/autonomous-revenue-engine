## Candidate body

We stopped arguing about whether a draft “sounds like us.”

We measure it.

Burrows’ Delta compares relative frequencies of high-frequency function words in a candidate against our Pass-only reference corpus. Lower Δ means closer stylistic match. High Δ plus flat sentence-length variation is an automation-risk flag before adversarial review starts.

Internal pair — same core idea, different production quality:

| Feature | Generic “before” (Δ high vs corpus) | Pass-style “after” (Δ low vs sample-a/b) |
|---------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Open line | “In today’s fast-paced world of content creation…” | “Kill the generic post before it leaves the desk.” |
| Function-word profile | Dense “the / of / and / to / is” filler stacks | Sparse fillers; concrete verbs and refusals first |
| Sentence-length CV | Flat ~12–14 words | Varied; short kill + longer mechanism |
| Approx Δ vs Pass corpus | ≥1.4 (far) | ~0.6–0.8 (close) |

Vibes do not survive a skeptical Premium Home Timeline. Delta does not write the post and does not publish. It only tells us when production drifted. The human still owns the claim.
