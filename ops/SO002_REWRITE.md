## Candidate body

Most dashboards still count every glance the same.

OCR does not. A qualified impression is a unique Premium user on the Home Timeline with ≥50% of the post visible. Replies do not count. Non-Premium does not count. Below the fold does not count.

Until the account meter is live we run this proxy template on every draft before originality scoring:

| Proxy field | How we log it (pre-account) | Why it maps to OCR |
|-------------|-----------------------------|--------------------|
| First-screen claim | Core refusal or mechanism lands in first 140–180 characters | ≥50% visible still carries value |
| Viewport hold | Would a time-poor Premium operator stop past the open line? | Skeptical Premium persona test |
| Audience fit | Angle aimed at operators who already pay for signal, not bait clickers | Premium/verified quality |
| Reply-primary risk | Designed to farm replies instead of Home Timeline hold? | Replies excluded from the 500k verified HT bar |

We do not invent revenue numbers. We only advance a draft when it clears this template and the originality gate. The scoreboard is the proxy until real Premium Home Timeline impressions exist.
