# Quality bar — 110% or nothing

**Locked after owner rejection of first Pass queue (2026-08-24).**

If a post is not something we would put our name on next to the best operators on X, it does not leave the building. “Good enough for a new account” is banned.

## Fail patterns (instant Kill)
- Abstract metaphor with no concrete stake (“timeline is a club”)
- Manifesto tone / sermon closer (“That is the job.”)
- Generic operator jargon with no lived detail
- Smooth model cadence: balanced triples, empty contrasts, tidy moral
- Teaching from zero authority without a specific observation or cost
- Anything that could be swapped onto any AI-growth brand and still “work”

## Pass requirements (all)
1. **Specific** — a real constraint, number, decision, or failure, not a category lecture
2. **Voice** — reads like a person under pressure, not a brand deck
3. **First screen** — the point is visible immediately; no throat-clearing
4. **Tension** — something is at risk, refused, or paid for
5. **Non-substitutable** — would not fit a random growth account
6. **Human-primary** — idea and angle from operator doctrine + owner context; AI is production only

## Standard
World-class for this lane = posts a sharp Premium reader would save or quote without cringing.  
If unsure → **do not post**. Rewrite or kill.

## Process implication
Old SO-001 / SO-004 / SO-007 Pass labels are **revoked**. Queue reset. No publish until new drafts clear this bar + adversarial review.
