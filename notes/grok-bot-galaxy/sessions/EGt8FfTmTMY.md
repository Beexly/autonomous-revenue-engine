## Use cases shown

- Morning status board — fires overnight, today's meetings, unfinished promises
- Call prep 15–20 minutes before a call — attendees and titles, last conversation, what launched since, suggested talking points, anything unblocked
- Follow-up desk — the moment a call ends: Granola notes and transcript to drafted replies and materials
- Promise keeper — what he said he'd do
- Ask watch — what he asked others to do and hasn't come back
- Account reset — a full download on an account, with a space joke to open because he only asks when he's stressed

## Takeaways the speaker stated

- Start with a voice dump — give the bot your context up front
- The 'imagination gap' is the real limit: the bot is capable, you underestimate it
- Put a bot in front — manage managers instead of ten bots
- Make it a living system: teach personality (his example: exclamation points, space jokes)

## Notes for the next agent

Best example of a self-improving setup: a weekly 'self-improvement scan' (Wednesday) audits the whole system for manual work that should be automated, and a voice-learning loop diffs every draft against what he actually sent, feeding the delta back to Wally. Routines run weekly 8:30, 09:00, 13:00 and a Friday dashboard.
