## Premise

The most opinionated session: four bots that run a founder's customer, product, competitive and feedback loops — ending with token-cost discipline and a shared bot.

## Agenda as presented

- AI maturity curve: chatbots, ephemeral agents, bots that compound, automating a staff function
- Why Grok Bot suits founders: preserve focus, maintain velocity, protect insight quality
- Four founder use cases
- Meet the squad and live demos
- Power-user tips (including token economics)
- Q&A plus a shared bot via QR code

## Use cases shown

- Closing customers — do things that don't scale, but scale them (activation credits example)
- Product changes — a daily rundown of what shipped AND unshipped, to avoid demoing a removed feature (he tells that story about himself)
- Adapting to competitors — a bot that stalks competitors for you
- Shipping feedback quickly — turn user feedback into PRs

## Bots / agents demoed

| Bot | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Closebot | Owns the customer: deep pre-call research (telemetry + site walkthrough), post-call compounding from Granola transcripts, support, activation |
| Prodbot | What shipped/unshipped: reads PRs and Linear issues, walks the product itself, returns screenshots and a video of the run, ties to metrics |
| Stockbot | The competition stalker — signs up for competitors' newsletters/products under its own research email, records onboarding walkthroughs, watches site/pricing/changelog/jobs/X (the same idea ships on the marketplace as 'Stalk Bot') |
| Protobot | Designs and turns feedback into PRs |

## Tools, integrations and surfaces named

Granola (call transcripts), Linear, PRs, routines (schedule/webhook/signal), cookies import, voice bot, marketplace

Frequent tool vocabulary in the transcript: `notion`, `calendar`, `mcp`, `api`, `teams`, `linear`, `salesforce`, `deploy`, `slack`, `drive`

## Takeaways the speaker stated

- Bots compound — keep them, invest in them, don't throw them away after a task
- Watch token economics: a 15-minute routine is 100 runs/day; prefer webhooks and inbound signals
- Make a voice bot; import your cookies; group bots by expertise
- Keep a bot whose only job is optimizing your other bots
