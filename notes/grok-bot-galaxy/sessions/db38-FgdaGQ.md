## Takeaways the speaker stated

- Scope each bot tightly — treat it like writing a job description with clear swim lanes
- Trust your bots: give access and ask them to study your context and propose what to take off your plate
- Invest in bots — feedback and memory compound over time
- The marketplace holds the team's best templates; he crowdsourced his demo bots from across the company
