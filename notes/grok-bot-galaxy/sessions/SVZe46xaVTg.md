## Use cases shown

- Pipeline generation (outbound prospecting bot)
- Answering engineering-specific customer questions without bugging the sales engineer
- Live call understanding and deck updates from discovery calls
- Chief-of-staff meeting prep (usable from a phone on the commute)
- Customer-expert tracking: usage before renewals, product signals, Slack threads, and matching shipped changelog entries back to the feature requests a customer made
- Forecasting as a one-click or routine-driven process
