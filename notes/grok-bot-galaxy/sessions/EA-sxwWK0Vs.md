## Use cases shown

- Self-completing task list — bots monitor every inbox, separate real tasks from FYI, and route work
- Cross-bot routing: a territory dispute handed to a territory-planning bot that knows the rules of engagement and the CRM, returning a first-draft deliverable
- Internal apps for marketing/sales teams where the rules are enforced by design
- Thinking through corner cases and product requirements for internal tools before building

## Takeaways the speaker stated

- You can hire an unlimited set of specialists with the right context and access — 'CEO life' democratized

- Your product is revenue growth; you are a PM for it
