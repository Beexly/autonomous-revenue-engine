## Takeaways the speaker stated

- Bots are meant to be multiplayer — share them so a whole team uses the same resources
- The 'ask rockbot' culture: when someone asks whether something is possible, the answer is often 'ask Grok Bot'
- Grok Bot pushes back and will say no — treated as an advantage over sycophantic models

## Notes for the next agent

Demonstrates the meta-pattern most clearly: he teaches one bot a skill, then asks it what teammates it needs — and the bot creates three working bots with defined interfaces to each other. Also the most explicit statement that an agent's willingness to refuse is a feature.
