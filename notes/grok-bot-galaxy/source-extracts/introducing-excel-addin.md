### Ask questions about your data

Select a range and ask Grok what moved, why, and what stands out. Answers cite the cells they came from, and charts can drop directly into the sheet beside the data.

#### Write formulas and run scenarios

Describe the outcome and Grok handles the syntax — averages, sorts, and fills land as normal edits in the formula bar. Test a downside case, rerun a forecast, or close out month-end without rebuilding the sheet by hand. The add-in can also leverage connectors; ask it to pull context from recent emails or files in SharePoint or Google Drive.

### Get started

Grok for Excel is a free Microsoft 365 add-in. Add it from the Microsoft Marketplace, and work directly with Grok in your workbooks. Also available for Word and PowerPoint.
